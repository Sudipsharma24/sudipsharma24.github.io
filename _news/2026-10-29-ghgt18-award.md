My e-poster, _Multi-Objective Optimisation of Magnetic Induction Swing Adsorption for Electrified CO₂ Capture_, received a **Student Poster Award** at the 18th IEAGHG Greenhouse Gas Control Technologies Conference ([GHGT-18](https://ghgt.info/)), held in Perth, Australia, on 25–29 October 2026. GHGT is the leading international conference series on carbon capture, utilisation and storage, organised by IEAGHG; this edition brought together around 1,100 delegates and over 400 e-posters.

## The work

In the accompanying lightning talk, _Electrifying Carbon Capture: Four Induction-Heated MOF Cycles, Simulated and Optimised_, I presented four magnetic induction swing adsorption (MISA) cycle configurations for the Fe₃O₄@HKUST-1 composite adsorbent. Each cycle is simulated with first-principles dynamic models in IDAES/Pyomo and optimised with Gaussian-process surrogates and multi-objective optimisation. In MISA, the adsorbent bed is heated directly by magnetic induction rather than by steam or hot purge gas, which opens an electrified route to sorbent regeneration.

The full analysis is published in [_Chemical Engineering Research and Design_ 234, 720–739 (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cherd.2026.09.029), with Prof. Thomas A. Adams II. The e-poster and the lightning-talk slides (PDF) are available on the [GHGT-18 e-poster stack](https://my.ltb.io/www/#/stack/ACQXD).
